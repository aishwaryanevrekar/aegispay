About

The human principal registers an ERC-8004 identity for the agent and issues a scoped session key: total cap, per-tx cap, expiry, payee lock, identity binding, and a single-use human approval for large payments. A settlement router enforces Confirmation of Payee, forwards tUSD8, and writes reputation receipts. The principal can revoke the key instantly.

Built from scratch by Aishwarya Nevrekar. Source: github.com/aishwaryanevrekar/aegispay
